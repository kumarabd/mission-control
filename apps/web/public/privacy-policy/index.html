Mission Control

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026. Mission Control (“we”, “us”) is operated by Nighthawk Labs at mission-control.nighthawklabs.org.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in, including with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile photo through our authentication provider (Clerk).
  • Content you give your companion. Messages, goals, reminders, memories and files you add, stored in your account’s workspace.
  • Data from services you connect. Only if you choose to connect a service (for example a Google account), and only the data your companion needs to act on your requests.
  • iPhone app permissions. If you allow them: the microphone (speech is transcribed on your device, and only the text is sent), the camera (to scan text or codes, or read a photo you choose), calendar (today’s events are shown beside your plan and stay on your device), Apple Health read access (to show your own summary on your device), and notifications (reminders and assistant messages). You can turn each off in iOS Settings.
  • Technical data. Basic logs such as IP address, device and browser type, used to keep the service running and secure.

How we use it

To authenticate you, run your companion and its memory, perform the actions you ask for, provide support, and keep the service secure. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

Google user data

Mission Control’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the user-facing features you requested, is not transferred to others except to provide those features, comply with law, or as part of a merger or sale with notice to you, is not used for advertising, and is not read by humans unless you ask for support, it is needed for security or abuse investigation, or the law requires it. It is not used to develop or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Gmail data

If you connect Gmail, we request read access (gmail.readonly) and draft-creation access (gmail.compose). Your companion uses them to search and read your email threads, manage labels, and create drafts when you ask. It does not send email on your behalf. Message content is processed to answer your request and is not stored beyond what you ask your companion to remember. You can disconnect Gmail at any time.

Sharing

We share data only with processors needed to run the service: authentication (Clerk), hosting and infrastructure, and the AI model provider you or the platform configures, which receives the context needed for each request (see “What leaves your space” above). We may disclose data if required by law.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can disconnect any connected service at any time, which revokes our access (you can also revoke it at myaccount.google.com/permissions). To delete your account and its data, email us and we will delete it within 30 days.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and access is authenticated. As described above, we do not claim end-to-end encryption.

Children

Mission Control is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.
